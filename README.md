# Breakup as a Service™ 💔

## Basic Details

### Team Name: Solo

### Team Members

* Team Lead: Johan Shibu John - College of Engineering Kallooppara

### Project Description

Breakup as a Service™ is an unnecessarily sophisticated AI-powered breakup processing system that treats a failed relationship like a corporate, legal, and criminal-level incident. From relationship audits and HR performance reviews to courtroom trials, autopsies, Malayalam final hearings, and absurd verdicts, the system makes breaking up far more complicated than it ever needed to be.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Breaking up is already difficult, but apparently the real problem is that people aren't receiving enough **official documentation, legal proceedings, HR evaluations, emotional damage reports, and courtroom judgments** for their failed relationships.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

We turned a simple breakup into a full enterprise workflow. Users submit their relationship details and the system generates an AI relationship audit, personalized roast, HR performance review, termination notice, legal charges, prosecution, defense, courtroom verdict, relationship autopsy, funeral, recovery report, breakup certificate, and a final Malayalam roast hearing.

Everything is completely free because **your relationship may have been expensive, but this service isn't.**
