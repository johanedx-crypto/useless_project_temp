3. ROAST ENGINE — MOST IMPORTANT FEATURE

==================================================

The roast engine is a CORE feature.

Do NOT produce generic motivational AI garbage.

Avoid:

“You deserve better.”

“It's time to move on.”

“Relationships are complicated.”

“Communication is important.”

These are boring.

Instead generate:

- personalized insults

- sarcasm

- absurd comparisons

- corporate insults

- legal insults

- courtroom insults

- HR language

- exaggerated statistics

- deadpan observations

- unexpected punchlines

- callbacks to information the user entered

The AI should use the user's actual answers.

Example:

INPUT:

“They reply after 12 hours.”

BAD:

“They reply slowly.”

GOOD:

“Communication efficiency has officially fallen below the response time of a government office.”

INPUT:

“They always say they are busy.”

GOOD:

“After extensive investigation, the court has discovered that the defendant appears to hold a full-time position at the Ministry of Being Busy.”

INPUT:

“They say ‘nothing’ when something is obviously wrong.”

GOOD:

“Subject repeatedly answered ‘nothing’ while producing enough emotional evidence to qualify as a national emergency.”

The roast should feel intelligent rather than randomly abusive.
